Principles

Calm infrastructure is designed, not discovered.

  1. 01

    Make failure domains visible.

    If the diagram cannot show what happens when a rack, a link, or a monitor disappears, the design is not finished.

  2. 02

    Prefer boring upgrades.

    A rollback plan, a tested migration, and a clear definition of done are worth more than a clever deployment.

  3. 03

    Make the system legible.

    Dashboards, runbooks, and diagrams are part of the product. The next engineer should not need the original author.
